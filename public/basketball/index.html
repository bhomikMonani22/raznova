How it works

Top picks: Stake player props whose price is at least the sharp books' fair price (Pinnacle, Novig, ProphetX, de-vigged), ranked by chance to hit; odds 1.30 to 3.00, one per player, two per game, ten a night. The parlay joins the best picks from three different games.

All props: Stake's main line for every player and market, scored for confidence: 70% the sharp fair chance and 30% recent form (last 5, 10, 15 and 20 games at that line, home or away, and against tonight's opponent, each pulled toward 50% so a few games don't count for much). Without a sharp price, or when the player is Questionable, the tier stops at Medium. The model column is our points model's chance.

Players: every player who has played for one of tonight's teams this season, and anyone on tonight's injury report. Tap one for his last 15 games against any line you set, season and home/away averages, his games against tonight's opponent, the opponent's defence against his position (last 20 games against the league), the game's pace and rest. All-Star games are left out.

For / against on a prop: context, not part of the confidence number. ✓ supports the lean, ✗ goes against it: his last-10 median against the line, his minutes over the last 5 games against the 15 before (3+ a game), the opponent's defence against his position (5%+ off the league) and the pace (2%+ off the league).

Games and team spread picks (G1, registered and frozen before the first pick, scored on 1 Dec by closing-line value): Stake's spread and total at every line against the sharp books' no-vig price at the same line (two or more of Pinnacle, Novig and ProphetX, from the hourly game lines). A pick is the best-value side of a game priced at or above fair (odds 1.5 to 3.0), recorded once a night at Rs 500 paper. The lean is the side of Stake's main line the sharp books make more likely; it is shown, not recorded. Tonight at the top sums up the night in plain sentences: tap one to open that player or game.

Paper edges (registered and frozen before their first signal, scored once on 1 Dec by closing-line value; display only until then): Injury model (E1), our frozen teammate-out model on tonight's official injury report, where it moves a player's points, rebounds or assists 8%+; Alt line (E3), Stake's ladder rungs priced from the sharp main line (5%+ value, odds 1.25 to 5); 1st quarter (E5), Stake's first-quarter props priced from the full-game sharp line and the player's first-quarter share; Blowout/rest (E4), starters' Unders when Stake's spread is 9.5+ or his team played yesterday. The fifth, Stake slow on late injury news (S2), is measured from the price histories, not shown here.

Roles: a player is flagged when his minutes moved 5+ a game against last season, or, with minutes steady, his share of shots moved 25%+ (3+ games this season, 20+ last season). Shown on his cards; the flag does not change any rating yet.

Learning: every pick and every rated prop is checked against the box score the next day; missed picks get a reason, and the tier table shows whether higher confidence actually hits more often.
